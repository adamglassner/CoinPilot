# WillowWallet for Mac and Linux — changelog

## 1.7.0 — 2026-11-17

- **Forecast.** Where your money is headed: your expected balance every day to the end of the month (or 30, 60 or 90
  days), from your paychecks, bills, budgets, debts and savings. It says whether you'll end over, short or right on
  target, shows the likely range and the lowest point, and what moves the number most. Try **what-ifs** (a one-time
  expense, extra money, skipping a bill, a different budget) without saving anything. Budgets you usually go over are
  forecast at what you usually spend.
- **Safe to spend**: how much you can spend beyond your budgets until the next payday, on the dashboard and Forecast.
- **Bill calendar balances**: each day shows your expected balance, and paydays show up too.
- **Sinking funds** (Goals): set aside a little each month for costs that come around every year, like car
  registration or holidays. "I paid it" starts saving for next time.
- **Spending alerts**, on the dashboard ("Heads up") and as notifications: a budget at 80% (or 70/90%), going over,
  a bill whose price changed (one tap updates the bill), unusually big charges, and a forecast dip. Settings →
  Reminders.
- **Paycheck planner** (Income): what each paycheck needs to cover until the next one, and what's left over.
- **This year vs last** and a **Monthly report** (your month on one page) on Reports.
- **Receipts**: scan a receipt and WillowWallet reads the store, total and date on your device. Every receipt is kept
  on the new Receipts page by year, and can be copied to Taxes.
- **Household**: name who shares expenses and how they're split, pick who paid each transaction, and see who owes whom.
- **Calculator**: Calculator in the sidebar (or ⌘K / Ctrl+K) opens a calculator beside it. Type on the keyboard;
  copy the result. On Linux, receipts are read with Tesseract when it's installed; on a Mac you type what the photo
  says.

## 1.6.1 — 2026-11-16

- Keeps WillowWallet for Mac and Linux on the same version as the other apps. The phone app can now choose its bottom
  bar pages; nothing changes here.

## 1.6.0 — 2026-11-16

The first version of WillowWallet for Mac and Linux, with everything WillowWallet for Windows does and a sidebar like
it: Dashboard, Transactions, Budgets, Accounts, Debts, Bills, Income, Goals, Reports, Taxes, Sync & Devices and
Settings.

- **Budgets** with rollover, one-month amounts, copy last month, templates (50/30/20, zero-based, starter), budget
  months that start on your payday, and **subscriptions you could cut** (Skip ▾ for this month, or for good).
- **Transactions** with transfers, splits, receipts, tags, rules, and importing bank statements (CSV, OFX, QFX;
  PayPal, Venmo and Cash App downloads recognized). **Accounts** and net worth, **Bills** with a calendar,
  **Income** and paydays, **Debts** with a payoff plan, **Goals**, **Reports** and year in review, **Taxes**.
- **Sync** with WillowWallet for Windows and Android through your WillowWallet Server, and **backups** in the same
  .zip as the Windows app (they restore on any of them).
- **Bill reminders** as notifications while WillowWallet is open, like on Windows. Turn on **Start when I sign in**
  (Settings → Reminders) to get them all day.
- **A PIN or password**, and **unlock with Touch ID** (Mac) or **your fingerprint** (Linux, with fprintd).
- **Updates from GitHub** in Settings → Updates. On Linux the AppImage replaces itself; on a Mac the new .dmg opens.
- Keyboard: Ctrl+N (⌘N on a Mac) adds a transaction; Ctrl+1…9 (⌘1…9) switch pages; Ctrl+, (⌘,) opens Settings.
